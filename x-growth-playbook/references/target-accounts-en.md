## 5. Avoid

- @sama / @elonmusk: thousands of replies per minute, your reply drowns in 5 minutes — unless the news is directly about your tools
- @karpathy: 1300+ replies of experts fighting; zero exposure for a small account — read only
- Pure repost bots: comment sections are just "noted / thanks for sharing", no discussion value
- Giveaway accounts, zombie mutual-follow rings: no real content, no real traffic, and they drag down your recommendation weight
- Anyone who blocked you: replies/likes get intercepted — remove from targets

## 6. Blue-check mutuals (real humans, can follow back)

Standard: blue check + real human (avatar, original posts) + actively posting + not a giveaway account → safe to follow / reply genuinely under their mutual-follow posts.
