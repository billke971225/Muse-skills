# Account Setup: Avatar, Bio, Persona

Do this once, before any posting. An account that looks like a bot gets treated like a bot.

## Avatar

- Real person's photo, face clearly visible, casual setting. Crop head-and-shoulders.
- No AI-generated faces, no logos, no abstract art. People follow people.
- Same photo (or same person) across X / GitHub / personal site — recognition compounds.

## Handle and display name

- Short, memorable handle. Your name or a stable nickname is best.
- Display name can be the human name; nickname in quotes if there's a story behind it.
- Don't change either after you start growing — links and mentions break.

## Bio (≤160 chars)

Formula: **who you are + what you do + why follow**.

Examples of the shape (write your own, in your own words):

- "Building in public. AI coding tools + indie SaaS. I test everything myself before I post."
- "Ex-founder turned indie hacker. Shipping one micro-SaaS a month. Real numbers, no guru talk."

Hard rules:

- No buzzwords: "passionate", "thought leader", "guru", "ninja", "enthusiast of innovation".
- No emojis-as-personality. One link max (your site or your best thread).
- Location: real city/country builds trust; leave blank only if there's a reason.
- **Ask the user to approve the bio before saving it.** Never invent their job title, company, or credentials.

## Pinned tweet

Pin one of these (in order of preference):

1. Your best long-form post (the one with real numbers and screenshots).
2. An intro post: who you are, what you'll post about, one honest line about where you are in the journey ("month 3 of building in public, $0 MRR so far").

## Persona rules (the voice behind the bio)

The persona is a **practitioner, not a teacher**. Someone who builds things themselves and posts what actually happened.

- Say "I tried this" more than "you should do this".
- Share numbers even when they're embarrassing. "$12 MRR, here's the breakdown" beats "how I made $10k" that never happened.
- Admit mistakes publicly. One "I was wrong about X" post earns more trust than ten hot takes.
- Never fake a milestone, a partnership, or a revenue number. One invented number kills the account permanently.

## First-week checklist

- [ ] Avatar, bio, pinned tweet set and user-approved
- [ ] Blue check active (verified)
- [ ] 5–10 real original posts live (voice-checked, see `voice-en.md`)
- [ ] Following 20–50 real accounts from `target-accounts-en.md`
- [ ] First reply session done (≥20 substantive replies)
- [ ] `seen_replies.json` / `liked.json` / `quoted.json` initialized
